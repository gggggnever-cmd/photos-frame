How to use
  1. In the YouTube app, open a video or playlist and tap Share → Copy link.
  2. Come back here, tap the box, choose Paste, then Play on TV.
  3. Your own playlists play if they're Public or Unlisted (not Private).
  4. A single song keeps going: similar songs (YouTube's mix) play after it.
  5. Keep this page handy: browser menu → Add to Home screen.
  6. If Chrome says the site can't be reached: phone Settings → Apps → Chrome → Permissions → Nearby devices → Allow.
