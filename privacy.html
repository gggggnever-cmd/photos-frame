Privacy Policy

Photos Frame is a personal, single-user app.

It accesses only the photos the user explicitly selects through the Google Photos Picker. Selected photos are downloaded to and stored locally on the user's own TV.

No photos or personal data are sent to the developer or any third party. There is no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

Google sign-in is used only so the user can pick their photos.
